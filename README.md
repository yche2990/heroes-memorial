# Heroes IV–VII Memorial (VCMI mod)

**英雄无敌4～7纪念版** — creatures, spells, abilities and adventure objects from *Heroes of Might and Magic IV, V, VI and VII* brought to [VCMI](https://vcmi.eu), as close to the originals as the engine allows.

## Contents

The main mod holds what the generations share (spell-effect scripts, the four Heroes VI battlefield statuses Chilled, Frozen, Soaked and Dazed, Stealth). Each generation is a submod that can be switched off on its own:

- **Heroes IV Memorial** — the Heroes IV creatures (stats from the Equilibris / EQEM edition) placed in the matching VCMI towns with their original abilities, the Heroes IV hero spells and potions, ten Heroes IV creature banks, the Memorial Apothecary, Magic Schools, Troop Exchanges and Sanctum (grail building blessings and the expansion divine artifacts), and the Heroes IV Elemental Conflux.
- **Heroes V Memorial** — 178 creatures (base, upgrade and alternative upgrade of every faction, plus neutrals) placed in the matching VCMI towns, with their original abilities, active skills and Blood Rage; 134 spells; the Memorial Magic Academies (three tiers), the Troop Exchanges and the Memorial Sanctum, whose rewards are the faction ultimates; the Heroes V Elemental Conflux.
- **Heroes VI Memorial** — 100 creatures, the bosses with their own boss treasuries, 204 spells (hero spells and creature active skills), racial Gating and Bloodrage, the Memorial Magic Academies, Troop Exchanges and Sanctum, and the Heroes VI Elemental Conflux. Tears of Ashan's Uriel boss gets the Heroes VI look and skills.
- **Heroes VI Dynasty Weapons** — 32 dynasty weapons in five levels each, given and upgraded by the Dynasty Weaponsmith, each with its original active and passive abilities.
- **Heroes VII Memorial** — 172 creatures, 109 spells, the Heroes VII runes, the Memorial Magic Academies, Troop Exchanges and Sanctum, the Heroes VII warfare units with the Memorial Blacksmith, and the Heroes VII Elemental Conflux.
- **Guarded Treasure Chests** — three kinds of chests on the adventure map, guarded by mixed armies of Heroes III–VII creatures.

Each generation also has a small **AI Assist** submod so computer players visit the memorial objects and value the new creatures and artifacts correctly; human players see no difference.

Languages: English (base) and Chinese. Players using any other game language get English.

Sprites are included at 1x only, and most images are stored as 256-colour PNGs with transparency to keep the download small; VCMI scales them up for higher interface scaling.

## Requirements

- VCMI 1.8 or later (the mod uses Lua battle scripts)
- [Tears of Ashan](https://github.com/vcmi-mods/tears-of-ashan) for Heroes IV–VII and the Dynasty Weapons: their spells and creatures use its light and dark magic schools, demon marker, Blood Rage, Gating and warcries
- [Highlands Town](https://github.com/vcmi-mods/highlands-town) for Heroes V and VII (the dwarves)

Optional: small compatibility submods load automatically when their target is enabled:
- Horn of the Abyss: Cove, Factory and Bulwark classes at the Dynasty Weaponsmith, Ring of Oblivion, Highlands and Wasteland terrains for Summon Elementals
- Tears of Ashan submods: Sanctuary (the Heroes VI Memorial Shrine accepts the Sanctuary faction), Cathedral (Inquisitors at the Heroes VII Memorial Blacksmith and the Dynasty Weaponsmith), Boss Encounters
- Highlands Town: its hero classes at the Dynasty Weaponsmith
- Heroes V Memorial: Sacred Ground of Heroes IV also blocks the Heroes V Animate Dead spells
